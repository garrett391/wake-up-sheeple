Proof

Wake upSheeple

The grass isn't greener. It's painted.

A playable demo. About 5 minutes, sound on.

How to play

You're a sheep at Greener Pastures Ranch. Find the glasses, find proof that the shepherds are wolves, and wake up 4 sheep. Then lead the flock to the gate.

    • Wolves only notice a sheep wearing glasses. When a red cone sweeps your way, graze with your head down or take the glasses off.
    • Proof only shows up while you wear the glasses. Ask each sheep what's bothering them, then show them the proof that fits. The wrong proof makes them sulk.
    • There are more pastures than the one you can see. Try the barn doors, and ask Kevin about Sunny Acres.
    • Getting caught or crying wolf costs Credibility. Lose all 3 and nobody believes you.
